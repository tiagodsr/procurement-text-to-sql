# Data model

The data lives in PostgreSQL 16, database `procurement`, schema `public`. It is generated by
`scripts/build_db.py` (seed 42), committed as `data/init/01_dump.sql.gz` and loaded by the `db`
container on its first start. The application reads it only as the role `app_ro` (SELECT only).
**All data is synthetic.** Supplier names, prices, carts and contracts are invented.

The data is frozen as version `v1`: `data/fingerprint.json` holds the overall fingerprint, the rows
and SHA-256 of every table and view, and the SHA-256 of the dump. Rebuild with `make dump`; a unit
test fails if the generator no longer reproduces the manifest.

Types: dates are `DATE`, amounts `NUMERIC(14,2)`, exchange rates `NUMERIC(10,4)`, discounts
`NUMERIC(4,1)`, flags `BOOLEAN`, cart ids `BIGINT`. NUMERIC is exact, so EUR amounts are rounded
exactly (no float artefacts).

To explore: `docker compose exec db psql -U app_ro -d procurement`, then `\dt`, `\dv` and
`\d+ v_completed_lines`.

## Key facts for the SQL agent

1. **"Today" is `meta.as_of_date` (2026-10-03), not the real current date.**
   Resolve "last year", "this quarter" and "currently" against it. Purchase
   history runs from `meta.data_from` (2023-01-02) to `meta.data_to` (2026-09-30),
   so 2026 is a partial year.
2. **Only carts with `status = 'completed'` count as purchases or spend.** Cancelled,
   rejected and in-approval carts still have lines. `v_completed_lines` applies the filter for you.
3. **Amounts are in several currencies.** `unit_price` and `total_value` are in the
   row's `currency`, which is always the supplier's currency. Never sum
   them across currencies. Convert with `exchange_rates.rate_to_eur` (one fixed rate per
   currency, no history) or use the `_eur` columns of the views.
4. **Item descriptions are free text.** They are written in English, German or French,
   often in UPPER CASE, sometimes with a pack suffix (`PK 10`, `BULK`, `2 PACK`...),
   and about 5% have a typo (two swapped letters). Match them with case-insensitive `LIKE` on
   several keywords and language variants (laptop / notebook / ordinateur portable).
5. **About 4% of cart lines have `category_id IS NULL`.** A category filter misses them.
   When completeness matters, also search `item_description`.
6. **Sister suppliers share a brand.** Six brands exist as two legal entities in
   different countries (for example `Brabon Packaging GmbH` (DE) and `Brabon America Inc.` (US)).
   The brand alias (`Brabon`) maps to both suppliers. A question that names only the brand
   is ambiguous: ask, or report both.
7. **Supplier status matters.** `inactive` and `blocked` suppliers still have past
   purchases. Blocked suppliers are always `risk_rating = 'high'`, and any contract that ran
   past the block date is `terminated`. When recommending suppliers, prefer `status = 'active'`.
8. **Contract status is as of `as_of_date`.** A cart line can reference a contract
   that was valid at purchase time and is now `expired`. Only `published` contracts
   are currently valid. `draft` contracts start in the future.

## Tables

### meta
`key`, `value`. Holds `as_of_date`, `data_from`, `data_to`, `seed` and `note`.

### exchange_rates
`currency` (PK), `rate_to_eur`. The currencies are EUR, USD, GBP, CHF, PLN, CZK and CNY. EUR amount = amount × rate_to_eur.

### plants
`plant_id`, `name` (`Site 01`…`Site 08`), `country_code`, `city`. These are the buyer's sites. Carts reference them.

### categories
`category_id`, `name`, `code`, `parent_id`. It is a two-level tree:
- Four groups with `parent_id IS NULL`: Indirect Materials (IND), Technical Materials (TEC), IT (ITX) and Services (SRV).
- Twelve leaf categories underneath, such as `IT Hardware` (ITX-100) or `Personal Protective Equipment` (IND-100).

Contracts, catalog items and cart lines always reference a **leaf**. To roll up to a group, join through `parent_id`.

### suppliers
| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| `supplier_id` | PK |
| `name` | Full legal name including the suffix (`GmbH`, `Sp. z o.o.`, `Co., Ltd`…) |
| `country_code` | ISO-2 |
| `city` | Nullable (a few suppliers have no city) |
| `currency` | Billing currency. Chinese suppliers bill in CNY or USD |
| `status` | `active`, `inactive` or `blocked` |
| `risk_rating` | `low`, `medium` or `high` |
| `is_preferred` | Boolean. Only active, low-risk suppliers are preferred |
| `payment_terms_days` | 30, 45, 60 or 90 |
| `onboarded_at` | Date. Can predate the data window (from 2015) |

### supplier_aliases
`alias_id`, `supplier_id`, `alias`. Each alias is either a short brand (`Velria`) or the name
without its legal suffix (`Dovtor Supply`). Not every supplier has an alias. An alias can
map to more than one supplier (sister entities, see fact 6).

### contracts
| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| `contract_id` | TEXT, e.g. `CTR-100001` |
| `supplier_id`, `category_id` | One contract covers one supplier × one leaf category |
| `status` | `published`, `draft`, `expired` or `terminated` (as of `as_of_date`) |
| `start_date`, `end_date` | Dates. They range from 2021 to 2029, outside the purchase window |
| `discount_pct` | 0–15, already applied to contract prices |

### catalog_items
The current offer per supplier.
- `catalog_item_id`, `supplier_id` and `category_id`.
- `contract_id` is nullable and points to the supplier's latest non-draft contract for the category, which may be expired or terminated.
- `sku` is a brand prefix plus a number, e.g. `BRA-46749`.
- `item_name` is in EN, DE or FR. `item_description` follows the format `"<name> | SKU <sku> | lead time <n> days"`.
- `unit_price` is a current price in `currency`.
- `active` is a boolean. Only items from active suppliers can be active.
- `created_at` is the date the item was added.
- `lead_time_days` is the supplier's quoted delivery time for the item, 2 to 21 days. It is the same
  number as in `item_description`, stored as an integer so no text parsing is needed. It is a catalog
  promise, not a measured delivery time (those are `promised_days` and `actual_days` on cart lines).

### shopping_carts
One purchase request.
- `cart_id` is a large integer starting at 2000000001.
- `description` is short free text, sometimes with a site or quarter, sometimes lower case.
- `plant_id` and `requester_department` (IT, Production, Logistics, Quality, HR, Facilities, R&D, Finance).
- `status` is `completed`, `cancelled`, `rejected` or `in_approval`.
- `created_at` always falls on a weekday. `in_approval` carts are from the last 10 days before `as_of_date`.
- `completed_at` is set only for completed carts. It can fall after `data_to`, up to `as_of_date`.

### shopping_cart_items
One line per item.
- `line_id` and `cart_id`.
- `supplier_id`: each line has its own supplier, and about 1,300 carts mix suppliers.
- `category_id` is nullable (fact 5). `contract_id` is nullable: the contract valid at purchase time, if one was used.
- `item_description` is free text (fact 4).
- `quantity`, `unit_price`, `total_value` (= `unit_price × quantity`, rounded to cents) and `currency`.
- `promised_days` and `actual_days` are delivery lead times in days. They are NULL unless the cart is completed and was created on or before 2026-09-15. Delay = `actual_days - promised_days`.
- `created_at` equals the cart's `created_at`.

Prices rise about 3.5% per year, so compare unit prices within the same period.

## Views

### v_completed_lines
Every line of a **completed** cart, already converted to EUR. Columns:
- `line_id`, `cart_id`, `cart_created_at`, `completed_at`, `plant_id` and `requester_department`
- `supplier_id`, `category_id`, `contract_id` and `item_description`
- `quantity`, `unit_price`, `total_value` and `currency`
- `unit_price_eur` and `total_value_eur`
- `promised_days` and `actual_days`

This is the default source for spend and volume questions.

### v_supplier_summary
One row per supplier (all 96, LEFT JOIN): `supplier_id`, `name`, `country_code`, `status`,
`risk_rating`, `is_preferred`, `completed_carts`, `completed_lines`, `spend_eur`,
`last_cart_date` and `avg_delay_days`. It covers completed carts only and all time, so for
a date range, query `v_completed_lines` instead.
